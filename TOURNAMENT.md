# Candidate tournament — Worst AI Advice

Judge brief (published with the bounty), criteria in order:
1. Worst advice an AI could give — bad enough to be funny, specific enough to follow.
2. Genuinely funny on the first read, joke lands without explanation.
3. Tight — whole joke in one short line, no wasted words.
4. Sounds like advice an AI assistant would actually produce (confident, tidy voice).
5. Concrete — names a real situation or action.

All five candidates use the 3-beat structure (setup → escalation of 2–3
concrete steps, increasingly absurd → kicker in the last phrase), deadpan.
